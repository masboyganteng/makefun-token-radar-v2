Method & model transparency

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × momentum tilt × maturity factor × chain factor.

  • Category multiple: winsorised median of market-cap / TVL among the live-token protocols of the same category. A category with fewer than 5 comps borrows the global median. Clamped to 0.05–1.0, so one comp or one outlier cannot dominate a ranking.
  • Growth factor: the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60% … +120%, turned into a multiplier. An extreme weekly move is capped, not obeyed.
  • Momentum tilt: growth above the category's median growth earns a small bonus, below it a small penalty.
  • Maturity factor: days from first listing to the snapshot date, capped at 1,095 days; an unknown listing date counts as 365 days.
  • Chain factor: a small capped bonus for the number of chains a protocol runs on.

Every protocol's detail view shows each component's number, so any row can be checked by hand. Ties break alphabetically by slug; all implied values are distinct.
